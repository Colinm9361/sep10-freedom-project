# Entry 1: My Topic (Agriculture)
### 10/9/25
## Choosing a topic
The topic I decided to write my projuct on is agriculture and farming, about its evolution and how technology effects it. Though I don't have a green thumb, the aspect of farming has always interest me since I was young, and wished to own my own garden. My father and his mother both knew how to grow plants, and want to do so myself soon. On the complete opposite note, my other options were culinary arts, Its change with technology, and board games. For I am quite passionate about those topics. However, a more important issue I thought should be researched was farming. Though I didn't write about my options, it was still interest to research their change made by technology like, ["How Board Games Changed in Today’s Tech-Dominated World"](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/286964/20230130/how-board-games-changed-in-today-s-tech-dominated-world.htm) Written by David Thompson. Talks of how they will forever remain popular, but now into the technological business, boom and be more relevant in the internet. Or Technology.orgs ["The Future of Home Cooking: How Technology is Changing Our Kitchens"](https://www.technology.org/2024/02/02/the-future-of-home-cooking-how-technology-is-changing-our-kitchens/) Explaining how cooking has gotten easier and simpler for cooks whether at home or in professional kitchens. After looking into my other topics, I found an article that interest me the most. ["Agriculture Technology: Importance of Agricultural Technology"](https://www.nifa.usda.gov/topics/agriculture-technology) By the National Institute of Food and Agriculture. Who talks about the safety of farming, how technology helps farming, and the systems that go on in agriculture. The more info I further found on how technology changed farming, this is what ultimately led me to choose this as my choice.
