## Research Topic: Agriculture and farming.
The topic I decided to write my project on is agriculture and farming, about its evolution and how technology effects it. Though I don't have a green thumb, the aspect of farming has always interest me since I was young, and wished to grow my own garden soon. My father and his mother both knew how to grow plants, and I wanted to do so myself soon. On the complete opposite note, my other options on this project were the culinary arts, Its change with technology, and board games. For I am quite passionate about those topics. However, a more important issue I thought should be researched was farming. Though I didn't write about my options, it was still interest to research their change made by technology like, ["How Board Games Changed in Today’s Tech-Dominated World"](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/286964/20230130/how-board-games-changed-in-today-s-tech-dominated-world.htm) Written by David Thompson. After looking into my other topics, I found an article that interest me the most. ["Agriculture Technology: Importance of Agricultural Technology"](https://www.nifa.usda.gov/topics/agriculture-technology) By the National Institute of Food and Agriculture. The more info I further found on how technology changed farming, this is what ultimately led me to choose this as my choice.
