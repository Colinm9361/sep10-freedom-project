## Part A: existing technology
Farming apps and softwares:
* My first google search was, "softwares farmers use".
  * The website I found was, [Local line](https://www.localline.co/blog/best-software-for-farmers)
  * This website had a list of 15 softwares farmers use for their projects, and an overview of what they were used for.
* App 1. Searched: [agrivi](https://www.agrivi.com/)
  * An app that could be used on your phone
  * Has a feature where farmers can look at diagnostic on their crops for good yield and harvest that best economically helps them.
  * Providing food traceability, with checking food retailers an esstimate lifecyle of fresh and processed from farm to table foods.
* App 2. [farmbrite](https://www.farmbrite.com/)
  * Has an organiser to organise your farm work and get things done faster and efficiently. (Even works offline)
  * Helps assign, manage, organise and prioritises tasks anywhere and everywhere.
  * Comes with an intergrated accouting finnancial planning for farmers, to check reports, cashflow and analisise to make taxes easier on farmers
  * Completely free, and works on any device.
* App 3. [local line itself](https://www.localline.co/suppliers)
   * The Local Line website provides a multitude of things for farmers to use.
     *  Resources for pricing and planning to sell farmer goods online.
     *  Sources to find suppliers, the ability to sell farmers crops on the website, and inventory management.
     *  Can be a website hub to buy crops for farming and yield
