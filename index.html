Agrilculture & Farming.

Context:

The building blocks for modern society was agriculture. What is agriculture? Agriculture is the process and science for cultivating plants, livestock, soil and farms. Since its first appearance roughly 700,000 years ago, is the building block for society. It is fundamental and has made major improvements since then. This websites purpose is to research and search for technology in the farming industry. How technology has influenced cultivation, what is being used, and how it can be changed in the future for the better.
